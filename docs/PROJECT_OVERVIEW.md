# CMRIT Placement PYQ Portal - Project Overview

## Introduction
The **CMRIT Placement PYQ (Previous Year Questions) Portal** is a web-based, student-focused platform built specifically for the students of the **Department of Computer Science & Engineering** at **CMR Institute of Technology (CMRIT)**. 

Placements are a critical milestone for engineering students, yet finding real, historical interview questions from past campus recruitment drives is often difficult. This portal aggregates real placement interview questions asked by various companies at CMRIT from **2019 to 2027**, serving as a centralized, interactive repository to help students prepare effectively.

---

## Core Value Proposition

* **Real Historical Data:** Access to authentic questions asked in past placement drives at CMRIT, rather than generic online lists.
* **Dual-Category Organization:** Clear distinction between **Technical** questions (theory, concepts, system design, databases) and **Programming** questions (coding problems).
* **AI-Assisted Learning:** Instant, context-aware interview coaching via LLM integration (Groq + Llama 3.1) directly on the portal.
* **Self-Paced Practice:** Persistent bookmarks to curate a customized revision list of challenging questions.

---

## Data Scope & Statistics
The platform currently organizes data containing:
* **Total Questions:** 1,000+ individual questions across technical and programming streams.
* **Company Representation:** Over dozens of prominent recruiters (e.g., PhonePe, Oracle, Amadeus, Eurofins, Deloitte, Ellucian, etc.).
* **Timeline Coverage:** Placement seasons ranging from the **2019-20** academic year up to **2026-27**.
* **General Practice Bank:** A dedicated fallback bank of core computer science questions for generalized placement prep.

---

## Key User Roles

### 1. CMRIT CSE Students (Primary Users)
* **Goal:** Practice, revise, and prepare for upcoming placement drives of specific companies.
* **Actions:** Search for questions, filter by company/year, view AI-generated answers, save questions for later revision, and ask the chatbot for general placement strategy tips.

### 2. Student Maintainers / Department Admins (Contributors)
* **Goal:** Update the question bank with questions from the latest placement season.
* **Actions:** Append new records to the structured JSON file (`questions.json`) to keep the portal up to date.

---

## Target Audience Context
Designed primarily for CSE branch students, the portal focuses on core areas typically queried by recruiters:
1. **Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA):** Recursion, Trees, Matrices, Strings, Linked Lists, etc.
2. **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP):** Pillars of OOP, polymorphism, interfaces, and language-specific details (Java, Python, C++).
3. **Core CS Subjects:** Database Management Systems (DBMS/SQL), Operating Systems (OS), Computer Networks (CN), and System Design.
4. **General Aptitude / Puzzles:** Mathematical puzzles, logical reasoning, and testing theory.
